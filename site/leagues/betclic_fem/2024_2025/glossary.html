Glossary

Definitions of the metrics used across the platform. Tip: on any page, hover a metric name with a dotted underline to see its definition.

Basics

GGames played
Number of games in which the player or team appeared.
MPGMinutes per game
Average minutes played per game.
PPGPoints per game
Average points scored per game.
RPGRebounds per game
Average total rebounds (offensive + defensive) per game.
APGAssists per game
Average assists per game.
SPGSteals per game
Average steals per game.
BPGBlocks per game
Average blocked shots per game.
+/-Plus/minus per game
Average point differential of the team while the player was on the floor.
Raw and noisy: depends heavily on teammates and opponents.
AST/TOAssist to turnover ratio
Assists divided by turnovers.
Above 2.0 is a strong ball-security profile for a playmaker.
Per 40Per 40 minutes
A counting stat scaled to 40 minutes of play (a full FIBA game), to compare players with different minutes.
Stat × 40 ÷ minutes

Shooting

FG%Field goal percentage
Made field goals divided by attempted field goals (twos and threes).
2PT%Two-point percentage
Made two-point shots divided by two-point attempts.
3P%Three-point percentage
Made three-point shots divided by three-point attempts.
FT%Free throw percentage
Made free throws divided by free throw attempts.
eFG%Effective field goal percentage
Field goal percentage adjusted for the extra value of three-pointers.
(FGM + 0.5 × 3PM) ÷ FGA
TS%True shooting percentage
Overall scoring efficiency, including twos, threes and free throws.
Points ÷ (2 × (FGA + 0.44 × FTA))
The best single number for scoring efficiency.
3PA RateThree-point attempt rate
Share of field goal attempts taken from three.
3PA ÷ FGA
A style metric: describes shot diet, not quality.
FT RateFree throw rate
How often a player or team gets to the free throw line relative to shot attempts.
FTA ÷ FGA
Pts/ShotPoints per shot
Points produced per field goal attempt.
Points ÷ FGA
3PM/GThree-pointers made per game
Average made three-point shots per game.
Shot AttShot attempts
Total field goal attempts with shot location data.

Advanced

USG%Usage rate
Share of team possessions used by the player while on the floor (shots, free throws and turnovers).
A role metric: high usage is not good or bad by itself.
AST%Assist percentage
Share of teammate field goals the player assisted while on the floor.
TOV%Turnover percentage
Turnovers per 100 possessions used.
TOV ÷ (FGA + 0.44 × FTA + TOV)
Lower is better.
ORtgOffensive rating
Points produced by the player per 100 individual possessions (Dean Oliver method).
Higher is better. Around 100–115 is typical.
DRtgDefensive rating
Estimated points allowed per 100 possessions while the player is on the floor (Dean Oliver method).
Lower is better. Heavily influenced by team defense.
NetRtgNet rating
Offensive rating minus defensive rating.
ORtg − DRtg
P (percentile)League percentile
Share of qualified players of the same league and season that the player outperforms (P85 = better than 85%).
For lower-is-better metrics (DRtg, TOV%) it is inverted, so a higher P is always better.

Rebounding & defense

ORB%Offensive rebound percentage
Share of available offensive rebounds grabbed while on the floor.
DRB%Defensive rebound percentage
Share of available defensive rebounds grabbed while on the floor.
TRB%Total rebound percentage
Share of all available rebounds grabbed while on the floor.
STL%Steal percentage
Share of opponent possessions that end in a steal by the player while on the floor.
BLK%Block percentage
Share of opponent two-point attempts blocked by the player while on the floor.

Impact & on/off

On Court %On-court percentage
Share of team minutes the player spent on the floor.
Min OnMinutes on
Total minutes the player was on the floor.
Min OffMinutes off
Total team minutes with the player on the bench.
Net On/40Net points on, per 40
Team point differential per 40 minutes with the player on the floor.
Net Off/40Net points off, per 40
Team point differential per 40 minutes with the player on the bench.
On-Off/40On-off net differential
How much better (or worse) the team's net result is per 40 minutes with the player on the floor than off it.
Net On/40 − Net Off/40
Context-dependent: needs a decent minutes sample to be meaningful.
WOWYWith or without you
Compares team results in four states for two players: both on, only A, only B, both off.

Team & lineups

OffRtgTeam offensive rating
Points scored per 100 possessions.
DefRtgTeam defensive rating
Points allowed per 100 possessions.
Lower is better.
PacePace
Estimated possessions per 40 minutes. Higher means a faster game.
PossPossessions
Estimated possessions.
FGA − ORB + TOV + 0.44 × FTA
Opp eFG%Opponent effective FG%
Effective field goal percentage allowed to opponents.
Lower is better.
Four factorsDean Oliver's four factors
The four team keys to winning: shooting (eFG%), turnovers (TOV%), offensive rebounding (ORB%) and free throws (FT Rate).
LineupLineup (five-man unit)
A group of five players on the floor together; ratings are per 100 possessions of that unit.
Small minute samples are unreliable; use the Min Minutes filter.
