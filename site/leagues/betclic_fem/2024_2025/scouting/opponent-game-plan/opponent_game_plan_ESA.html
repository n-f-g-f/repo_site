How They Play

Game Plan in 4 Keys

  • Control the rhythm: they are more comfortable in a controlled half-court game, so do not let them play cleanly into structure.
  • Win the first action: they do not consistently hurt teams with second-chance volume, so clean first stops carry value.
  • Know the first reference: D. Rouse is a movement shooter, and the plan should start with taking away his comfort.
  • Closeout discipline matters: D. Rouse, R. Stasova are the first perimeter names you cannot lose in help situations.

  • Priority closeouts start with D. Rouse, R. Stasova, and M. Umabano; do not lose them in help-and-recover situations.
