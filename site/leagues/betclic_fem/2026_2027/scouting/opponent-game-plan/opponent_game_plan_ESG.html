How They Play

Tempo
Above-average pace; expect a game that can speed up if you allow early flow.
Ball Movement
Ball movement is close to league average; they can share it but are not purely pass-first.
Offensive Glass
Limited offensive rebounding impact; finishing the first contest usually kills the possession.
Turnovers
Turnover profile is close to league average; pressure helps, but it is not the whole plan.
Physicality
Above-average foul volume; contact pressure and discipline both matter in this matchup.
Shot Mix
Above-average three-point volume; spacing discipline and clean closeouts matter.

Game Plan in 4 Keys

  • Set the tempo early: if you let them run into flow, the game can speed up on their terms.
  • Win the first action: they do not consistently hurt teams with second-chance volume, so clean first stops carry value.
  • Stay connected to spacing: their perimeter volume can bend the floor if your help and recovery are sloppy.
  • Know the first reference: G. Raimundo is a primary scorer, and the plan should start with taking away his comfort.

Scouting Notes

Offense

  • The main offensive references are B. Souza and L. Rodrigues; load the scouting plan into those names first.

Perimeter Shooting

  • Priority closeouts start with G. Raimundo, B. Souza, and A. Marques; do not lose them in help-and-recover situations.
  • Some perimeter volume comes from A. Marques and B. Souza; contest, but do not over-help into secondary advantages.

Scoring Areas

  • Their offense naturally flows toward Above the Break 3; protect that area early in possessions.
  • Several players have clear comfort zones, especially A. Marques, L. Rodrigues, and B. Souza; force them away from preferred areas.
