Power Rankings

Teams ranked by Power: net rating per 100 possessions adjusted for the strength of the opponents faced, so beating strong teams counts more. SOS = strength of schedule (rank 1 = hardest). Proj W-L adds the win probability of every remaining game to the current record.

Method: per-game net rating, iteratively adjusted for each opponent's own adjusted rating (simple rating system), centred on 0 and shrunk toward 0 early in the season. Rankings are computed within each regular-season phase, because teams of different groups do not meet. SOS left accounts for home and away. Win probabilities use power, pace and the league's observed home advantage. Movement (±) compares with the ranking before the latest round.
