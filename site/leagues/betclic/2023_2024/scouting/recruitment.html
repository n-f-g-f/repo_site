Recruitment · Profile search

Find players by profile across the leagues in your plan. Percentiles (P) compare each player with qualified players of the same league and season (≥10 MPG and ≥5 games, lower early in the season), so profiles are comparable across competitions. For DRtg and TOV% the percentile is inverted: higher P is always better.

Profile conditions

Each condition keeps players at or above a league percentile, e.g. AST% ≥ P75 = top 25% passers of their league. Players without percentiles (below the minutes/games threshold) are excluded when a condition is active. Age, height and nationality come from the roster; players without them are excluded only when that filter is used.
