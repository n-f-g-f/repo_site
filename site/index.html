What PBA does

Three connected surfaces designed the way basketball is actually studied.

Analytics

Deep league analytics

Team and player advanced dashboards, on/off impact, lineup ratings, playmaking profile, four factors by quarter and matchup matrix. Data organized the way coaches actually read the game.

Scouting

Full team scouting

Team Hub as the central entry point, plus Opponent Game Plan, Opponent Shot Report, Visual Shot Dashboards, Game Script Profile and Rotation Timeline. Everything you need to prepare a real opponent, in one place.

Player evolution

Career evolution, not just stats

Player Hubs with advanced production and impact metrics, plus historical pages focused on what actually changed in each player's career: role, scoring, efficiency and league percentile evolution.

Organized by league and season

The platform is structured the way you already think about basketball.

Common questions

Short answers. The full FAQ lives on the Access page.

Which leagues are included?

Betclic (top men's league), Betclic Fem (top women's league) and Proliga (second men's league). Your plan defines which of these you can open.

Do plans include historical data?

Yes. Access to a league always includes the full historical archive of that league.

How does access work?

After activation, you log in and the platform opens the leagues included in your plan. Direct links to protected pages redirect to login when needed.

How do I get started?

Contact us to choose a plan and activate your account. Activation is normally completed within 24 business hours.
